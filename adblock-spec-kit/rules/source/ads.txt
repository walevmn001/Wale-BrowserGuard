! --- B2b-1: generic ad subdomain prefixes (third-party sub-resources only) ---
! Public ad-microsite convention, self-authored for this project (MIT). The
! trailing dot keeps the anchor on a label boundary, so `||ads.` matches
! `ads.<anything>` without also matching e.g. `adserver.com`; `$third-party`
! leaves a site's own first-party requests alone and, with no `resourceTypes`,
! the rules never match main_frame (see rules/README.md).
||ads.$third-party
||ad.$third-party
||adserver.$third-party
||adx.$third-party

! --- B2b-2: vendor ad-server endpoints (third-party sub-resources only) ---
! Vendor-specific hostnames the generic `<prefix>.` rules above cannot reach
! (their first label is not a known ad word). Public vendor endpoints,
! self-authored for this project (MIT). Domain-anchored and, with no
! `resourceTypes`, never applied to main_frame (see rules/README.md).
||an.yandex.ru^
||adfox.yandex.ru^
||adfstat.yandex.ru^
||bat.bing.com^
||gemini.yahoo.com^
