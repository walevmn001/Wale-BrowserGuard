! Cosmetic (element-hiding) filters — starter set for P2 (plan.md §3, algorithm.md §D).
!
! Only the subset in scripts/build-cosmetic.ts is converted:
!   ##selector · domain##selector · #@#selector · domain#@#selector
! Procedural rules (#?#, #$#, #%#) and `~`-exclusion domains are skipped and
! reported at build time, never guessed at.
!
! Selectors stay narrow (class / id / attribute anchored). Bare tags and `*`
! are rejected as too broad, so the build can never hide a whole page.

! --- Generic ad slots (class or id anchored) ---
